# Codebook for derived summary inputs

## clean_tables_for_main.xlsx
- Main manuscript table inputs, including stacked and first-window hospitalization estimates.

## supplement_tables_clean.xlsx
- Supplementary table inputs, including trajectory-specific crude rates, annual adjusted odds ratios, overall annual hospitalization rates, and year-by-trajectory summaries.

## Key variables
- `N`: number of respondent-year observations or participants, depending on the table.
- `Events`: number of 12-month hospitalization events.
- `Rate_pct` or `Rate (%)`: crude or survey-weighted rate in percent, as indicated by the table.
- `OR_vs_Q1`, `LCL`, `UCL`: adjusted odds ratio and confidence interval versus Q1.
- `traj` or `Trajectory`: life-space mobility trajectory group.
